# TrustLens Lite: Product

## Audience
Non-technical office staff who have just selected text in an email or document and want to know what to do with it. They are not reading to learn how the tool works. They want a next step.

## Job
Show four checks (Bias, Accuracy, Deception risk, Governance) and one recommended action, in plain English, readable in under 10 seconds.

## Voice
Calm, careful, hedged. "Signals, not proof." A person makes the final call.
Say "potential concern", "unverified", "source unclear". Never say "fake", "lie" or "deceptive". Buttons are verbs. Errors say what happened and what to do next.

## Feel
Trustworthy and institutional, not startup. The judges are records and information management professionals.

## Guiding idea
The report reads like a careful records officer's margin notes on the document: quoted evidence on a left rule, a short note beside it, a clear stamp at the top. It must not look like a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Success test
A first-time viewer can say what to do next within 5 seconds of the stamp appearing.

## Not in scope
Sidebar, top navigation, charts, sign-in, other browsers (see SPEC.md section 9). SPEC.md section 9 lists history as out of scope; the History list added here stores only the action, decision and time, never the text.
