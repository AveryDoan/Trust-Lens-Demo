## The script, as one read

A customer sends a long, angry complaint about an invoice. Three attachments. Normally, someone reads all of it, and checks every claim by hand.

TrustLens Lite opens beside the email, in one click.

It asks first. Personal details are masked on this device before anything is sent.

First, an assessment: four checks.

One-sided wording. It generalises about a whole group.

The email claims a five-day refund. The agreement says ten.

"Emailed many times" needs checking. The thread shows three.

It reads the attachments too, starting with the invoice.

Line by line, the invoice is eleven thousand, eight hundred and eighty dollars above the agreement.

The file details raise a flag. A clue, not proof.

Only now, the next step: approve the refund, with the rule it comes from.

A dated path: who, and by when.

Ask the accounts team what they received: the claim beside the thread. Guidance, not policy.

TrustLens Lite. Every action quotes the rule it comes from.

## Notes for the recording

- **Dollar amounts:** say "eleven thousand, eight hundred and eighty dollars" in full. Do not read the symbols.
- **"Five-day" and "ten":** the screen says 5 and 10 business days. Say "business days" if you want to be exact, for example "five business days, the agreement says ten". That adds two words to that block.
- **Pauses:** leave a beat after "four checks", after "the thread shows three" and before "Only now". The camera moves at those points.
- **Wording:** the script says "raise a flag", "needs checking" and "a clue, not proof", not "fake" or "fraud". The product shows signals and a person makes the final call, so keep the voice hedged too.
- **Demo data:** if you want to say it aloud, add at the very end: "Demo data. All names and numbers are fictional." It is already on the end card, so this is optional.
- **Timing:** if a block runs long, cut the first sentence of S1 ("Three attachments.") or the "Only now," in S6. The scene lengths do not change.
- **The permission screen:** the "It asks first" line describes the mock-up in S3. The real extension does not show that screen today, so change that line if you want the voiceover to describe only what is built. For example: "Personal details are masked on this device before anything is sent."
